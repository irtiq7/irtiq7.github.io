Projects

Software, demos, and experiments alongside my research.

  • Contour Synth

    Draw on a grid and hear it: every line you draw becomes a melody played by its own instrument, with scales, keys, tempo and a smooth-glide mode. Built with the Web Audio API.

  • Creative Prompt AI

    An open-source desktop GUI for generating images with text-to-image models from Hugging Face, with LoRA support and batch generation.

  • Pitch estimator

    A browser demo that identifies the fundamental frequency of an instrument.

  • Interactive AI periodic table

    An interactive periodic-table-style overview of AI concepts.

  • Daily AI news

    Research, technology and business news from public feeds, rebuilt daily. Each story has a short extract, reading time, topics and a source rating (left to right), plus search, filters, saved stories and topic trends. Signals adds emerging ideas, topic momentum and a public forecast scoreboard.
