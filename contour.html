Contour Synth

Draw a line and it becomes music. Each line you draw is a contour: left to right is time, and higher on the grid means a higher note. Every contour gets its own instrument, and they all play together in a loop.

Next instrument:

    How it works

    • The grid is a loop of two bars, divided into 32 sixteenth-note steps. The playhead moves from left to right and starts again at the end.
    • The horizontal lines are the notes of the chosen scale, over three octaves; the highlighted ones are the key note. A line's height at each step picks the note, and steps that stay on the same note are joined into one longer note.
    • Each contour gets the next instrument (Glass, Reed, Flute, Pad, Pluck, Brass), so several contours become a small ensemble. Up to eight contours play at once.
    • Turn on Smooth glide to hear the exact shape of each line, sliding between notes instead of stepping.
    • Press the space bar to play or stop. Without a mouse or touch screen, use Random contour.
    • Everything runs in your browser with the Web Audio API. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
