Draw Music

Draw with the pen and press play. The height of your ink is the pitch, and left to right is time. Every line you draw is its own voice with its own sound, and the notes snap to the key and scale you choose, so it always sounds in tune.

Runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Your drawing stays on this device.

    How to use it, and what you are hearing
    • Draw: press and drag with the pen. Fine, slow strokes give fine detail. The height of the ink is the pitch and the position across is the time; the loop repeats.
    • Every line is its own voice: by default each new line takes the next colour, and each colour has its own sound. Change a colour's sound, or a single line's sound in the list below.
    • Locked to key or freehand: locked, your line is read in steps of the quantize length and snapped to notes of the scale, so there are no wrong notes. Freehand lets the pitch slide smoothly along your line.
    • Key and scale: drag across the keys to change the key, even while it plays. Switch from major to minor, dorian or phrygian and hear the same drawing change its mood. The darker rows are the tonic, the “home” note.
    • Quantize and swing: quantize is the shortest note. Triplets divide a beat in three instead of two, and swing delays the off-beat notes so a loop feels looser.
    • Keys: space plays and stops, P is the pen, E the eraser, G the grid, and Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes.
