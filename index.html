Usama Saqib

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the SQUARE group at the IT University of Copenhagen (ITU). I received my Ph.D. in signal processing from Aalborg University, Denmark, in 2021, after an M.Sc. in Embedded Systems Engineering (2015) and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (2011).

My research lies at the intersection of statistical signal processing, robot audition, and autonomous systems. I develop model-based and learning-based methods that let robots perceive their surroundings through sound, with a focus on acoustic echoes and reflector localization for spatial map construction.

Research

Echo estimation

How can a robot recover where sound reflections come from?

I estimate times and directions of arrival of acoustic echoes using model-based statistical methods, including expectation-maximization, even in the presence of the robot's own ego-noise.

EURASIP J. Audio, Speech, Music Process. · 2024

Robust Acoustic Reflector Localization using a modified EM algorithm

With M. G. Christensen and J. R. Jensen

Spatial mapping

Can echoes alone build a map of a room?

I turn estimated echoes into acoustic reflector positions and spatial maps for robotic platforms, a topic that was also the subject of my Ph.D. thesis.

Robotics and Autonomous Systems · 2021

A Framework for Spatial Map Generation using Acoustic Echoes for Robotic Platforms

With J. R. Jensen

Learning

What can machine learning add to model-based acoustic perception?

I explore machine learning frameworks for acoustic reflector mapping that complement the statistical models developed during my Ph.D.

Preprint · 2024

A Machine Learning framework for Acoustic Reflector Mapping

With L. Marchegiani and J. R. Jensen
